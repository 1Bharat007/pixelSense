# Notification Center & Trust Experience

## Implementation Status
- **Status:** Fully Implemented
- **Component:** `Notifications.tsx`
- **Data Source:** Real JSONL Backend Data (`notifications.jsonl` parsed via `get_notifications` IPC command).

## Notification Center
The notification center provides a non-invasive, persistent list of alerts and recommendations generated by the intelligence engine.
- **Priority Grouping:** Visual hierarchy separates High Priority (e.g., Sensor Disconnects) from Normal Activity (e.g., Optimizations).
- **Actionability:** Notifications contain explicit contextual actions (e.g., "Retry", "Dismiss") rather than vague text.
- **Search & Filtering:** Users can search past alerts.
- **Smart Suppression:** "Dismiss All" immediately cleans the UI state.

## Trust & Transparency
By surfacing every background decision in the History and Notification engines, we eradicate the "black box" feeling.
- Users never wonder "Why did my brightness change?" because a Notification or History log explicitly states: "We reduced your brightness by 15% to match your room."
- The `Overview.tsx` gracefully falls back to explicit, human-readable explanations when sensors fail, rather than silently degrading or spamming error codes.
