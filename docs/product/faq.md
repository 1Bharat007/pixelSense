# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## 1. Why is my brightness changing constantly?
This usually occurs if "Screen Content Analysis" is enabled while you rapidly switch between dark and light applications. You can disable this or change the "Transition Speed" in Settings.

## 2. Does PixelSense send my screen data to the cloud?
No. As stated in the onboarding flow, all screen analysis happens 100% locally on your machine. We do not transmit or record your screen.

## 3. Why did PixelSense pause while I was watching a movie?
PixelSense detects fullscreen applications (games/movies) and temporarily pauses brightness transitions to prevent distractions. This behavior can be changed under `Settings > Fullscreen Media`.

## 4. How do I backup my settings?
Go to `Settings > System > Configuration Backup` and click Export. You can restore these settings on any device running PixelSense.
