# Project Vision

## Why PixelSense Exists
Display technology is advancing rapidly, but operating systems often lag in providing unified, intelligent control over brightness, color accuracy, and visual comfort. PixelSense bridges this gap by offering a single, cross-platform interface that respects user privacy and works entirely offline.

## Target Users
- Developers seeking precise, quick control over multiple displays.
- Professionals working in dark or light-sensitive environments.
- Anyone looking for a privacy-respecting alternative to cloud-connected display tools.

## Engineering Philosophy
See our [Engineering Principles](../development/engineering_principles.md) for details on our technical approach. At our core, we believe in **Local First, Native Performance, and Strict Privacy**.

## Long-Term Goals
- Seamless multi-monitor brightness and contrast synchronization.
- Time-based and ambient-aware adaptive adjustments.
- Color profile management across different operating systems.
- Robust plugin ecosystem for power users.

## Non-Goals
- We will not implement cloud sync or user accounts.
- We will not track telemetry or usage data.
- We will not bundle bloatware or heavy electron runtimes.
